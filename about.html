About HAM Club

Connecting Passion Wirelessly

What is Amateur (HAM) Radio?

Amateur Radio, also known as HAM Radio, is a worldwide communication service used by licensed operators to communicate, experiment with wireless technologies and provide emergency communication.

About HAM Club

The HAM Club encourages students to learn wireless communication, electronics, antenna design and technical innovation through workshops, practical sessions and engineering projects.

Our Vision

To inspire students through technical learning, innovation and research in wireless communication technologies.

Our Mission

To provide students with opportunities to learn, build and innovate through Amateur Radio, electronics and communication technologies.

Our Motto

Learn • Communicate • Innovate
